# Feature Specification: AI's Impact on K-12 Classroom Efficiency

**Feature Branch**: `1-ai-k12-efficiency`
**Created**: 2025-12-21
**Status**: Draft
**Input**: User description: "Research paper on AI's impact on K-12 classroom efficiency

Target audience: Education administrators evaluating AI adoption
Focus: Teacher workload reduction and student outcome improvements

Success criteria:
- Identifies 3+ concrete AI applications with evidence
- Cites 8+ peer-reviewed academic sources
- Reader can explain ROI of classroom AI after reading
- All claims supported by evidence

Constraints:
- Word count: 3000-5000 words
- Format: Markdown source, APA citations
- Sources: Peer-reviewed journals, published within past 10 years
- Timeline: Complete within 2 weeks

Not building:
- Comprehensive literature review of entire AI field
- Comparison of specific AI products/vendors
- Discussion of ethical concerns (separate paper)
- Implementation guide or code examples"

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Research Paper for Education Administrators (Priority: P1)

An education administrator needs to understand the evidence-based impact of AI on K-12 classroom efficiency to make informed decisions about AI adoption in their district.

**Why this priority**: This is the primary use case - education administrators are the main target audience who need to evaluate AI adoption and understand ROI.

**Independent Test**: The research paper can be independently evaluated by having an education administrator read it and subsequently explain the ROI of classroom AI to a colleague.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an education administrator reading the research paper, **When** they finish reading, **Then** they can articulate specific ROI metrics for AI adoption in K-12 classrooms
2. **Given** a need to justify AI investment, **When** the administrator references the research paper, **Then** they can cite evidence-based benefits related to teacher workload reduction and student outcomes

---

### User Story 2 - Evidence-Based AI Applications Analysis (Priority: P2)

An education researcher needs to understand specific AI applications that have demonstrated impact on K-12 efficiency based on peer-reviewed research.

**Why this priority**: Secondary audience of researchers and decision-makers who need concrete examples of AI applications with evidence.

**Independent Test**: The paper can be independently validated by having an external reviewer verify that at least 3 concrete AI applications are identified with supporting evidence from peer-reviewed sources.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a researcher reviewing the paper, **When** they examine the AI applications section, **Then** they find 3+ concrete AI applications with supporting evidence
2. **Given** a need to validate claims, **When** they check citations, **Then** they find peer-reviewed academic sources published within the last 10 years

---

### User Story 3 - Comprehensive Research Paper with Academic Rigor (Priority: P3)

A stakeholder needs a comprehensive research paper that meets academic standards while remaining accessible to education administrators.

**Why this priority**: Ensures the paper meets both academic rigor requirements and accessibility for the target audience.

**Independent Test**: The paper can be independently validated by checking that it contains 8+ peer-reviewed sources, is 3000-5000 words, and follows APA citation format.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a quality review process, **When** the paper is evaluated, **Then** it contains 8+ peer-reviewed academic sources with proper APA citations
2. **Given** a word count requirement, **When** the paper is measured, **Then** it contains between 3000-5000 words

---

## Edge Cases

- What happens when research findings are contradictory across different studies?
- How does the paper handle AI applications that show mixed results in different educational contexts?
- What if the most recent peer-reviewed sources don't fully support the claims being made?

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Paper MUST identify at least 3 concrete AI applications with evidence of impact on K-12 classroom efficiency
- **FR-002**: Paper MUST cite at least 8 peer-reviewed academic sources published within the past 10 years
- **FR-003**: Paper MUST enable readers to explain the ROI of classroom AI after reading
- **FR-004**: Paper MUST support all claims with evidence from peer-reviewed academic sources
- **FR-005**: Paper MUST be between 3000-5000 words in length
- **FR-006**: Paper MUST use Markdown format for source documentation
- **FR-007**: Paper MUST use APA citation format for all academic sources
- **FR-008**: Paper MUST focus on teacher workload reduction and student outcome improvements
- **FR-009**: Paper MUST be accessible to education administrators without technical background
- **FR-010**: Paper MUST exclude ethical concerns and product/vendor comparisons

### Key Entities

- **Research Paper**: The primary deliverable containing analysis, evidence, and conclusions about AI's impact on K-12 efficiency
- **Academic Sources**: Peer-reviewed journals and publications that provide evidence for claims made in the paper
- **AI Applications**: Specific implementations of artificial intelligence that demonstrate impact on educational efficiency
- **ROI Metrics**: Quantifiable measures that demonstrate return on investment for AI adoption in K-12 settings

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: Education administrators can articulate at least 3 specific ROI metrics for AI adoption after reading the paper
- **SC-002**: Paper contains exactly 8+ peer-reviewed academic sources published within the last 10 years
- **SC-003**: Paper is between 3000-5000 words in length with proper academic formatting
- **SC-004**: At least 3 concrete AI applications with evidence are identified and explained
- **SC-005**: All claims made in the paper are supported by peer-reviewed academic evidence
- **SC-006**: Paper is completed and delivered within 2 weeks of project start
